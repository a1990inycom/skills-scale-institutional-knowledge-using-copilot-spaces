 ## Roles and Personas
 
 OctoAcme defines clear accountability across the project lifecycle:
 
 - Project Manager (PM): coordinates schedules, risks, communication, and documentation.
 - Product Manager / Product Lead: defines outcome, prioritizes work, and measures value.
 - Developers: implement features, write tests, and contribute to design and reviews.
+- Program Delivery Manager: coordinates dependencies and workstreams across teams.
+- Risk and Dependency Coordinator: tracks risks, blockers, and escalation paths.
+- Business Value / Customer Advocate: ensures customer and stakeholder outcomes remain central.
+- Release Readiness Lead: prepares launch readiness, communication, and operational handoff.
 - QA / Testing: validate quality and acceptance criteria.
 - Stakeholders: provide input, approval, and business context.
