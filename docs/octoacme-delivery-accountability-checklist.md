# OctoAcme Delivery Accountability Checklist

Use this checklist during planning, execution, and release readiness reviews to confirm ownership, handoffs, and decision-making clarity.

## Planning Check
- [ ] Business goal and success criteria are documented
- [ ] Product owner or Product Manager has confirmed priorities
- [ ] Project Manager has confirmed timeline, milestones, and owners
- [ ] Dependencies are identified and assigned
- [ ] Risk and mitigation owners are named
- [ ] Stakeholders have been informed of expected outcomes and cadence

## Execution Check
- [ ] Each workstream has a named owner
- [ ] Status updates are current and visible
- [ ] Risks and blockers are logged and reviewed
- [ ] Dependencies are tracked and escalated when overdue
- [ ] Design or technical decisions are documented
- [ ] Hand-offs between teams are explicit and acknowledged

## Release Readiness Check
- [ ] Release Readiness Lead has confirmed launch criteria
- [ ] Acceptance criteria and definition of done are complete
- [ ] Operational support and rollback plan are in place
- [ ] Stakeholder communication plan is approved
- [ ] Launch risks have been reviewed and residual risk is understood
- [ ] Support and escalation contacts are identified

## Decision and Escalation Guidance
- [ ] Escalation path is known for risks, blockers, and missed commitments
- [ ] Product trade-offs are reviewed with Product Lead and stakeholders
- [ ] Schedule impacts are communicated to the Project Manager and affected teams
- [ ] Leadership is informed when cross-functional risk cannot be resolved within the team

## Ownership Summary
- Project Manager: plan, timeline, communication, and status
- Product Manager: value, priority, and outcomes
- Developers: delivery, quality, and implementation
- Risk and Dependency Coordinator: escalation and risk visibility
- Program Delivery Manager: cross-workstream dependencies and program alignment
- Release Readiness Lead: readiness, launch checks, and operational handoff
