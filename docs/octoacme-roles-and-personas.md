# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Program Delivery Manager

### Role Summary
Program Delivery Managers coordinate work across multiple workstreams, teams, and milestones so that broader initiatives stay aligned to business goals and delivery commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Manage dependencies across workstreams and stakeholders
- Monitor progress against program-level milestones and outcomes
- Coordinate prioritization and trade-offs when multiple teams compete for capacity
- Ensure cross-functional execution remains aligned to the agreed roadmap
- Escalate decisions, blockers, and resourcing gaps to leadership when needed

### Goals
- Deliver strategic initiatives predictably and with clear accountability
- Reduce friction between teams and stakeholders
- Maintain alignment between portfolio priorities and execution realities

### Typical Communication
- Program-level status reviews and dependency checkpoints
- Cross-team planning and roadmap alignment meetings
- Leadership updates on milestones, risks, and delivery health

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to sequence milestones and align delivery plans
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure the roadmap remains feasible and customer value is preserved
- Coordinates with Developers and other delivery teams to resolve dependencies and avoid bottlenecks

---

## Risk and Dependency Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Risk and Dependency Coordinator owns the identification, tracking, and escalation of project risks, constraints, and cross-team dependencies so issues are resolved before they impact delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain an up-to-date risk register and dependency log
- Identify potential blockers, single points of failure, and escalation triggers
- Facilitate issue resolution by connecting stakeholders with owners
- Support decision-making with clear risk impact and mitigation options
- Ensure recurring risks are visible across project reviews and governance forums

### Goals
- Reduce uncertainty and operational disruption
- Improve early visibility into delivery risks
- Ensure teams have a clear escalation path for issues

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and mitigation planning sessions
- Dependency tracking across delivery and stakeholder groups
- Escalation notes and governance updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to prioritize and escalate risks in project plans
- Works with Developers and Product Managers to assess trade-offs and mitigation options
- Keeps leadership and stakeholders informed of material impacts on timeline, scope, or quality

---

## Business Value and Customer Advocate

### Role Summary
The Business Value and Customer Advocate ensures that delivery decisions continue to reflect customer needs, business outcomes, and stakeholder priorities throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Represent customer and stakeholder needs during planning and trade-off discussions
- Validate that scope, quality, and delivery decisions support intended outcomes
- Help prioritize features and fixes based on customer impact and business value
- Support user feedback loops and success measurement efforts
- Clarify decisions when project constraints require trade-offs

### Goals
- Protect customer value and business outcomes
- Ensure product decisions remain grounded in user and stakeholder needs
- Improve acceptance and adoption of delivered solutions

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and customer feedback reviews
- Prioritization workshops and roadmap discussions
- Outcome and value tracking reports

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to validate priorities and success metrics
- Partners with Project Managers and Delivery Managers on trade-offs that affect scope, timing, or quality
- Provides context to Developers and testers so work reflects customer and business needs

---

## Release Readiness Lead

### Role Summary
The Release Readiness Lead coordinates the final preparation for deployment, validation, and operational handoff so launches are predictable and well-supported.

### Responsibilities
- Validate release readiness against quality, compliance, and operational requirements
- Coordinate launch checklists, communication plans, and go-live approvals
- Lead readiness reviews with engineering, operations, and stakeholders
- Manage launch dependencies and contingency planning
- Ensure support and rollback plans are in place before production release

### Goals
- Reduce release failures and avoidable disruption
- Improve launch confidence and stakeholder readiness
- Support a smooth handoff from delivery to operations

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and readiness review meetings
- Launch communications and approval gates
- Post-release support follow-up and incident coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Project Managers to confirm delivery status and launch criteria
- Collaborates with Product Managers and stakeholders to align release timing and communication
- Coordinates with operations and support teams to confirm readiness for ongoing service ownership

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Additional roles can be added as project complexity grows, especially when cross-functional delivery, stakeholder alignment, and release governance require clearer accountability.
